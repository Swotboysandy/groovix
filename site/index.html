Groovix

A music player for Android. Stream millions of songs, follow along with synced lyrics, and listen together with friends.

What it does

  • Search and stream songs, albums and artists.
  • Time-synced lyrics from several lyric providers.
  • Recommendations and an endless AutoPlay queue.
  • Listen together: share a code and play the same music in time.
  • Optional Google sign-in to bring your YouTube playlists, likes and subscriptions into the app.
